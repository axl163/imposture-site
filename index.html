Imposture

Say one word. Don't blow it.

What it does

  • Pass one iPhone around the table.
  • Most players see the secret word. One or two Impostors see a hint instead, and they do not see each other.
  • The word comes from food, things, places, or famous people. Players can write their own words instead.
  • Each player says one word before the TNT hits zero, then taps I'm done.
  • Everyone who still has a fuse points at who they think the Impostor is.
  • Names, points, and past games stay on this iPhone.

Privacy

Imposture keeps player names, points, and past games on the iPhone so the leaderboard can continue. Imposture does not create an account, and it does not send that information to a server.
